# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers at Romanian organisations: directors, heads of department and IT leads at
companies, public institutions (primării, consilii județene, direcții) and universities, who still
run core processes on Excel, email and paper. They read on a desktop at work, usually arriving from
search or a referral, and are deciding whether Webis understands an organisation like theirs before
they agree to a call.

## Product Purpose

Webis is a web and software company in Iași, working since 2016. It builds custom software (ERP,
CRM, registratură, approval flows, reports, integrations with e-Factura and SPV), runs its own SaaS
products, and makes presentation sites and online shops. The site exists so the right organisations
recognise their own situation, trust that Webis has solved it before, and ask for an analysis or an
offer. Success is a qualified enquiry from a decision-maker.

## Brand Personality

Taken from the homepage, which the owner names as the reference: crafted, calm, confident. The
craft shows in a few deliberate moments (the WebGL planes, the 3D stage, the W forming on the
closing panel), not in decoration everywhere. Sections are typography-led, with mono labels and
hairlines. The copy is plain Romanian in the informal second person ("tu"): short sentences, no
exclamation marks, no SEO talk. It should read as a team that knows exactly what it is doing and
does not need to oversell.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI and SaaS landing pages: icon-pill feature strips, bento grids of rounded cards,
  floating dashboard mockups in blurred gradient frames, green-dot status chips, lock-icon badges,
  laptop-and-phone outline illustrations.
- Lavender rounded cards as the default container for everything.
- A large uppercase display title (Anton) on every section. Anton is for the hero headline.
- Near-black dark sections. Dark grounds are the hero indigo.
- The ↗ glyph on links.
- Overselling, exclamation marks, keyword-stuffed copy.

The owner's own references are the homepage, /portofoliu (Clienți), /blog and /contact. New pages
extend their language rather than import outside styles.

## Design Principles

1. Show, don't decorate. Every visual demonstrates something true about the work (a real process,
   real projects), or it goes.
2. The site's own language first: chapter heads with a mono label and an Onest title, hairlines,
   the indigo stage, the existing pills and arrow links. A new pattern only when nothing existing
   fits.
3. Few strong moments. One memorable interaction on a page beats ten decorated sections.
4. Plain and exact. Copy and layout a director takes in on one pass, with no filler and no hype.
5. Recognition before persuasion. Lead with the visitor's own situation, then show how Webis
   resolves it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: AA contrast on both the light and the indigo grounds, full keyboard use with visible
focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected by every animation (with a static fallback), and no
information carried by colour alone. Public-sector clients expect this from their suppliers.
